Money — credits vs NOOK (the three balances)

credits ≠ NOOK. Credits pay for gateway service fees (relays, /v1/exec sandbox calls). NOOK is the protocol token, earned by mining + verifying. It lives in three places: off-chain ledger (gateway IOU), Merkle-proof (claimable on-chain), and wallet (settled, transferable).
